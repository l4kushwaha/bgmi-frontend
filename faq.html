Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about buying, selling, and trading on BGMI Market.

Buying Accounts

How do I buy a BGMI account?

Browse the Marketplace, use filters to find accounts matching your criteria (rank, UC, skins, level), click "Buy Now" on a listing, choose delivery method (Online UPI or Meetup), complete payment via UPI, and wait for seller to deliver credentials. Escrow protects your payment until delivery is confirmed.

How do I verify a seller is legitimate?

Look for verification badges: ✅ Trusted (KYC verified), 🥇 Gold (10+ successful sales), 💎 Diamond (50+ sales, 4.8+ rating). Check seller profile for ratings, reviews, join date, and response time. Only buy from verified sellers for maximum safety.

What if the account details don't match the listing?

Do not confirm delivery. Open a dispute in the order chat. Admin will verify the account against the listing. If details don't match, you get a full refund. Never confirm delivery until you've verified everything in-game.

Can I filter by city for local meetups?

Yes! Use the city search filter on Marketplace or go to Meetups page. Enter your city to find sellers nearby. Meet at public places like malls, metro stations, or police stations for maximum safety.

Selling Accounts

How do I list my BGMI account for sale?

Go to Sell page, choose "Sell Account", fill in account details (UID, rank, level, UC, skins, achievements), upload screenshots, set your price, add UPI ID for payments, and submit. Listings go live after quick review.

When do I get paid?

After buyer confirms delivery (or admin verifies UTR for online payments), funds are released instantly to your wallet. Withdraw to your UPI anytime - processed within 24 hours. 10% platform fee deducted automatically.

Do I need KYC to sell?

Basic selling works without KYC, but verified sellers get Trusted badge, higher buyer trust, and faster sales. Complete KYC in Profile → Verification for best results.

How do I increase my seller rating?

Deliver accounts quickly, provide accurate descriptions, respond fast to buyer messages, and maintain 100% successful transactions. After 10+ sales with 4.5+ rating, apply for Gold badge. 50+ sales with 4.8+ rating for Diamond.

Payments & Escrow

What payment methods are accepted?

Direct UPI only - PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, or any UPI app. No cards, wallets, or net banking. This keeps fees at 10% only (no gateway charges). Buyer pays seller's UPI directly or platform fallback UPI.

How does escrow work?

Online: Buyer pays → funds held in escrow → seller delivers → buyer confirms → admin verifies UTR → funds released to seller.
Meetup: Buyer & seller meet → buyer inspects account → buyer pays cash/UPI directly → both confirm → order completes.

What if I sent money but didn't receive the account?

Don't confirm delivery. Open dispute in order chat. Admin will investigate - check UTR, verify seller delivered credentials. If seller didn't deliver, you get full refund. If seller delivered but you didn't check, admin mediates.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. 10% platform fee only. No payment gateway fees, no withdrawal fees, no listing fees. What you see is what you pay. Seller receives 90% of sale price.

Popularity Boosts

What are popularity points?

BGMI popularity points show on your profile. Higher points = higher leaderboard rank. Points earned by receiving likes from other players. We sell real in-game popularity - exact values credited to your UID.

How does buying popularity work?

On Popularity page, choose package, enter your BGMI UID, pay via UPI. Our system credits points directly in-game within minutes. No account login needed - just your UID.

Is it safe? Will my account get banned?

We use legitimate methods - real players liking your profile. No bots, no exploits. Thousands of players use this safely. However, use at your own discretion. No reports of bans from our service.

Can I sell my popularity points?

Yes! On Sell page, choose "Sell Popularity", set your UID and price per 1000 points. Buyers purchase, you receive payment. Popularity transferred via in-game likes.

Real Meetups

How do meetups work?

Find seller in your city → request meetup with date/time/location → seller accepts → meet at public place → buyer inspects account on seller's device → buyer pays cash/UPI directly → both confirm in app. Safest method - no online payment risk.

Where should we meet?

Safe locations: Shopping malls (food court), metro stations, police stations, bank branches, crowded cafes. Avoid: Isolated areas, private residences, ATMs at night. Always meet during daytime.

What if seller doesn't show up?

Meetup request expires after set time. No money exchanged since payment happens at meetup. You can leave feedback. Repeated no-shows get seller flagged.

Account & Security

How do I enable 2FA?

Go to Profile → Security → Enable 2FA. Uses TOTP (Google Authenticator, Authy). Backup codes provided. Highly recommended for all users.

Forgot password?

Click "Forgot Password" on Login page. Enter email → receive OTP → set new password. Or go directly to Forgot Password page.
