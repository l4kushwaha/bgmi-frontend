Safety Guide

Trade with confidence. Follow these guidelines to protect yourself from scams and fraud.

⚠ Golden Rules - Never Break These

  • Never pay outside the platform. No direct UPI to seller before escrow, no crypto, no bank transfers to personal accounts.
  • Never share your BGMI account credentials before payment is secured in escrow or you're at a meetup inspecting the account.
  • Never confirm delivery until you've logged in and verified everything (rank, UC, skins, email access).
  • Never rush. Scammers create urgency. Take your time to verify.
  • Never meet at isolated locations. Only public places during daytime.
🛡️

Before You Buy

  • Check seller verification badge (Trusted/Gold/Diamond)
  • Read all reviews and ratings on seller profile
  • Verify account details match screenshots
  • Ask for recent gameplay video if unsure
  • Use platform chat for all communication
  • Confirm seller has valid UPI ID listed
💳

Payment Safety

  • Always use platform escrow for online deals
  • Pay only to seller's listed UPI or platform fallback
  • Save UTR screenshot immediately after payment
  • Never send "advance" or "token" money
  • Don't pay via QR codes from chat images
  • Report payment issues instantly in order chat
🔍

Account Verification

  • Log in and check: Rank, Level, UC, Skins, Achievements
  • Verify email is unlinked or accessible
  • Check for banned/restricted status in-game
  • Test login from different device if possible
  • Change password & enable 2FA immediately
  • Unlink seller's social accounts if any
🤝

Meetup Safety

  • Meet at: Malls, Metro stations, Police stations, Banks
  • Daytime only (10 AM - 6 PM)
  • Bring a friend if possible
  • Inspect account on seller's device before paying
  • Pay cash or UPI only after verification
  • Both confirm in app before leaving
📱

Communication Security

  • Keep all chat on platform - don't move to WhatsApp/Telegram
  • Use in-app voice messages for clarity
  • Screenshot important agreements
  • Report suspicious links or requests
  • Don't share OTPs with anyone
  • Verify admin identity - they never ask for passwords
🚨

Red Flags - Walk Away If:

  • Seller refuses escrow or meetup
  • Price too good to be true (90% off)
  • Pushes for quick decision/urgency
  • Asks for payment to different UPI
  • No screenshots or blurry images
  • New account with expensive listings
  • Refuses to show account live
